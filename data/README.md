# Test data

- `GTM_Engine.xlsx`: the full four-tab sheet, ready to upload to Google Drive and open as a Google Sheet.
- `target_accounts.csv`: six real public SaaS companies, so the research agent has real websites and news to read.
- `mock_leads.csv`: ten invented contacts designed to land in every routing tier. The people are fictional; some use real company domains only so the lead scoring workflow can match them to account briefs. Nothing in these workflows sends email to them.
